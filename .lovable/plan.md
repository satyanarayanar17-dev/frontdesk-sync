# Callwoven logo and homepage refinement

## Visual direction
- Use the original curved, interwoven green-and-copper C/W logo rather than the current angular substitute. Extract and clean the supplied logo, preserving its recognisable shape and texture; update the website icon to match.
- Give the logo a balanced horizontal position, consistent margins and more breathing room beside the name and navigation.
- Keep Instrument Serif headings, Work Sans body text and an illustrated opening, as selected.
- Use a calm mineral-white, eucalyptus, pale sage and muted blush page palette; preserve green and copper in the logo.

## Homepage
- Take inspiration from FrontDesk Assist’s clean navigation, service-focused hierarchy and generous section spacing—not its photos, claims or testimonials.
- Replace the oversized decorative ribbon with a clear illustrated enquiry-to-follow-up flow and a compact fictional enquiry summary.
- Keep “A clear next step for every call.” and the existing supporting copy and pilot offer.
- Give Dental and Trades equal, clearly labelled sections, followed by the proposed pilot process, concise benefits, enquiry form and FAQ.

## Preserve and check
- Preserve prices, links, owner access and the existing enquiry submission pipeline. No publication or DNS changes.
- Check logo readability, desktop/tablet/mobile layouts, contrast, keyboard access and enquiry feedback.

## Technical details
- Update shared marketing presentation and homepage styling only, with a matching favicon asset.
- Reuse the supplied logo artwork rather than generating a different logo.

## Preview limitation
The generated design options repeatedly changed the agreed text, colours and service claims. Those options are rejected; this plan follows your actual choices instead.
